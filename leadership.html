Leadership

Called to shepherd, serve, and build.

Dwell Ministries is led by Wallace and Erin Ford, who answered God’s call to create a Christ-centered online community of worship, prayer, discipleship, family strengthening, and compassionate service.

Pastor Wallace and Executive Pastor Erin Ford

Meet our pastors

Leading together with faith, love, and purpose.

Wallace and Erin Ford co-founded Dwell Ministries as a family-centered online ministry and continue to serve together in pastoral, strategic, and operational leadership.

Their shared commitment is to steward God’s call faithfully, care for people compassionately, build strong families, and create sustainable pathways for spiritual growth and community impact.

WF

Pastor Wallace Ford

Lead Pastor & Co-Founder

Pastor Wallace provides spiritual and executive leadership for Dwell Ministries. His heart is to preach God’s Word faithfully, shepherd people with compassion, strengthen families, and help believers grow into mature disciples who live with purpose.

After years of ministry preparation and study, Wallace embraced the pastoral call confirmed through prayer and the spiritual guidance of Apostle Dr. Craig Harrison and Prophetess Karen Harrison.

EF

Executive Pastor Erin Ford

Executive Pastor, Director of Operations & Co-Founder

Executive Pastor Erin provides ministry, strategic, and operational leadership for Dwell Ministries. Her work connects vision with execution, develops ministry systems, supports families and leaders, and advances Dwell’s digital ministry and long-term community-impact vision.

Erin brings a heart for prayer, discipleship, excellence, compassionate service, and building sustainable structures that help people flourish.

Spiritual formation

Prepared through study, covering, and obedience.

Wallace and Erin studied for more than nine years through Saturday Morning Seminary under Apostle Dr. Craig Harrison. Their pastoral call was affirmed under the spiritual covering and guidance of Apostle Dr. Craig Harrison and Prophetess Karen Harrison of The Inspired Word Church. They were ordained on June 7, 2025.

Together they are committed to faithfully stewarding Dwell Ministries, caring for God’s people, rightly dividing Scripture, strengthening families, and demonstrating the love of Jesus through both spiritual ministry and practical service.

Leadership at Dwell

Dwell’s developing leadership model values biblical integrity, spiritual maturity, safeguarding, financial accountability, operational excellence, collaboration, and responsible board oversight.
