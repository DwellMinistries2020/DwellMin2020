Accessibility

A digital ministry where everyone can belong.

Dwell Ministries is committed to making our website and online ministry experiences understandable, usable, and welcoming for people with disabilities.

Statement updated November 18, 2026

Our accessibility commitment

We aim to follow the principles of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as we improve the website. This is an ongoing commitment rather than a claim that every page or third-party service is fully conformant in every situation.

Accessibility features currently provided

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus indicators
  • Skip links that move directly to main content
  • Responsive layouts for phones, tablets, and desktop screens
  • Large, high-contrast action buttons, including mobile Join Live access
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Semantic headings and labeled navigation regions
  • Text that can be enlarged with browser or device settings
  • Reduced-motion support for people who request it through their device settings

Third-party platforms

Some ministry activities use Zoom, WhatsApp, Instagram, Tithe.ly, Cash App, Marriott, or other independent services. Their accessibility features and limitations are controlled by those providers. We will try to help identify an alternative way to access essential ministry information when reasonably possible.

Alternative access

If a digital barrier prevents you from joining worship, requesting prayer, giving, receiving conference information, or contacting the ministry, please tell us what you were trying to access and the format or assistance that would help.

Report an accessibility barrier

When contacting us, it is helpful to include the page address, a description of the problem, your device or browser if known, and the accommodation or alternative format you prefer. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis or private medical information.

Accessibility support: dwellministries2020@gmail.com

Continuous improvement

We review accessibility as pages, links, images, events, and ministry services are added. Feedback from people with disabilities and their families helps us prioritize meaningful improvements.
