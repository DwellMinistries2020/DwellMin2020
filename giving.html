Generosity

Partner with the mission.

Giving helps Dwell Ministries sustain online worship and prayer, disciple believers, strengthen families, develop outreach, and responsibly prepare for future community-impact initiatives.

Why we give

Generosity is worship and stewardship.

We give in response to God’s grace and as faithful stewards of what He has entrusted to us. Every gift should be handled responsibly, transparently, and in furtherance of Dwell’s religious, charitable, and educational mission.

Future giving opportunities may support digital ministry, conferences, discipleship, family and youth development, compassionate outreach, and the long-term Dwell HUB vision.

“So let each one give as he purposes in his heart, not grudgingly or of necessity; for God loves a cheerful giver.”2 Corinthians 9:7 NKJV
