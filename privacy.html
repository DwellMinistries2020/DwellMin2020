Privacy

Privacy with care and integrity.

We respect the trust you place in Dwell Ministries and aim to handle personal information with care, transparency, and responsible stewardship.

Effective November 18, 2026

How we handle information

This Privacy Policy applies to dwellmin.online and information you choose to share with Dwell Ministries through links and communications originating from this website.

Information you may provide

You may choose to share your name, email address, prayer request, connection inquiry, conference interest, giving-related question, or other information when you contact us. Dwell Ministries does not sell personal information provided through this website.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to you, provide prayer or pastoral care, share ministry or event updates you request, maintain ministry records, support safety, and meet legal or administrative responsibilities.

Prayer requests and pastoral care

We aim to treat prayer requests and pastoral-care information with discretion. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised when someone may be at risk of harm, abuse is suspected, disclosure is legally required, or consultation is necessary to provide responsible care.

Giving and payments

Online contributions are processed by Tithe.ly or Cash App. The Dwell Ministries website does not directly collect or store your complete payment-card or bank-account information. Those providers process information under their own privacy and security practices.

Third-party services

Our website links to services including Zoom, WhatsApp, Instagram, Marriott, Tithe.ly, and Cash App, and—with your permission—uses Google Analytics. When you use independent services, their own terms and privacy policies apply. Dwell Ministries does not control how independent third parties collect or use information.

Cookies, analytics, and technical information

Dwell Ministries uses Google Analytics only after you choose Accept analytics. Analytics may collect limited usage and technical information, such as pages viewed, link interactions, approximate location, browser and device type, and visit timing. We use this information in aggregate to understand which ministry resources are most helpful and to improve the website.

We configured Analytics without Google signals or advertising personalization and do not use it to serve ads. Prayer requests, contact-form answers, names, email addresses, phone numbers, and message content are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics. Form-interaction measurement is disabled.

Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it. Choosing Only necessary prevents the Google Analytics tag from loading. You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy choices button displayed on the website. Google processes Analytics data under its own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of ministry communication platforms and contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

Your choices

You may accept or decline optional website analytics and change that choice at any time using the Privacy choices button. You may also ask us to review, correct, or delete information you previously provided, subject to safety, legal, financial-recordkeeping, or ministry-administration requirements. We aim to retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Policy updates

We may update this notice as our website, ministry activities, or legal responsibilities change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
