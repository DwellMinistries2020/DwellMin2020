New to Dwell?

Your first visit can be simple.

Join from wherever you are, keep your camera and microphone off if you prefer, and participate only when you feel comfortable.

Join the Next ServiceSee What to Expect

Before you join

No pressure. No special preparation.

1

Open Zoom

The Join Live button opens our Zoom gathering. Your device may ask whether you want to use the Zoom app or continue in a browser.

2

Choose your comfort level

Your camera and microphone may remain off. You do not have to speak, introduce yourself, or appear on camera. You are welcome to simply listen and worship.

3

Participate when ready

Use the Zoom chat, worship with us, or unmute during an invited testimony or creative-expression opportunity when you feel comfortable.

Participate your way

There is room for your voice—and your quiet presence.

Chat is welcome

You may send messages to everyone in the Zoom chat. “Hallelujah,” “Praise the Lord,” Scripture, encouragement, and other respectful, Christ-centered responses are welcome.

Testimony and creative expression

When the testimony or creative-expression portion is opened, you may unmute and share a testimony, song, poem, or other Christ-centered expression to the Lord. Our service leaders will help guide the time so everyone is served well.

What Sunday may include

Worship, Scripture, prayer, and community.

  • Welcome and opening prayer
  • Worship and Christ-centered creative expression
  • Scripture and biblical teaching
  • Testimony and prayer ministry
  • Announcements and opportunities to connect
Service times: Sunday Worship at 11:30 AM Eastern and Tuesday Night Prayer at 7:30 PM Eastern.

Respecting privacy online

Please do not record, photograph, or screenshot participants without permission. Avoid sharing private medical, financial, family, school, home-address, or identifying information in the public chat.

Parents and guardians should supervise minors participating online. Private prayer and pastoral-care requests may be submitted through our secure connection form.

Need immediate help?

Prayer and pastoral care have important boundaries.

Dwell Ministries offers prayer and pastoral support but does not replace emergency, medical, mental-health, legal, or protective services. Call 911 for immediate danger. Suspected abuse or neglect in Florida may be reported to the Florida Abuse Hotline at 1-800-962-2873.
