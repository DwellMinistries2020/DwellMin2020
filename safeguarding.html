Safeguarding & care

Safe people. Healthy boundaries. Compassionate care.

Dwell Ministries is committed to honoring the dignity, safety, and well-being of children, youth, vulnerable adults, families, volunteers, leaders, and every person who participates in our ministry.

Safeguarding information

Our commitment

Abuse, neglect, harassment, exploitation, grooming, sexual misconduct, intimidation, and retaliation are inconsistent with the values of Dwell Ministries. Concerns should be taken seriously, handled promptly, and addressed with the safety of those at risk as the first priority.

This page provides public safeguarding expectations and reporting information. Activity-specific procedures may also apply to online gatherings, youth ministry, conferences, pastoral care, volunteers, or in-person events.

Children and youth

Adults and leaders should use transparent, ministry-appropriate communication with minors. Parents or guardians should be included when reasonably appropriate. Secret relationships, grooming behavior, sexualized communication, requests for intimate images, and pressure to hide communication from a trusted adult are prohibited.

Online ministry

Participants should avoid posting private contact information, school information, home addresses, medical details, or other sensitive information in public chats. Parents and guardians should supervise minors’ accounts and participation based on age, maturity, and the platform being used.

Photos and recordings

Images, livestreams, screenshots, and recordings involving minors or vulnerable participants should be handled carefully and only for an appropriate ministry purpose. Parent or guardian permission should be obtained when required, and private pastoral conversations should not be recorded without clear permission.

Pastoral care boundaries

Prayer and pastoral support are not substitutes for emergency services, licensed medical or mental-health care, legal advice, or protective-services intervention. Confidentiality may be limited when safety, suspected abuse, or legal reporting responsibilities are involved.

Report a concern

Safety comes before reputation.

If a child, vulnerable adult, or any person is in immediate danger, call 911. Do not delay emergency or protective-services reporting while waiting for a ministry response.

In Florida, known or suspected abuse, neglect, abandonment, or exploitation involving a child or vulnerable adult should be reported to the Florida Abuse Hotline at 1-800-962-2873, available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Outside Florida, contact the appropriate local law-enforcement or child/adult protective-services agency.

Florida Abuse Hotline InformationNotify Dwell Leadership

What to do when you are concerned

  1. Move away from immediate danger when it is safe to do so.
  2. Contact emergency services or the appropriate reporting authority when required.
  3. Record what you directly observed or were told without conducting your own investigation.
  4. Preserve relevant messages, dates, names, screenshots, or other information.
  5. You may also notify Dwell leadership so ministry access, communication, or participation can be addressed.

Reporting a concern to Dwell Ministries does not replace a report to law enforcement or protective services. Information may be shared on a need-to-know basis with appropriate leaders, insurers, legal advisers, platform providers, or authorities when necessary for safety and responsible response.

Good-faith reporting and non-retaliation

No person should be punished or intimidated for raising a genuine safety concern in good faith. Deliberately false or malicious allegations are also inconsistent with ministry integrity.
